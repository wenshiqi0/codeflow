# Handoff Contract

A handoff gives one specialist enough context to produce one bounded outcome. The body is semantic; Codeflow CLI owns state transitions.

Include sections that carry meaning for the receiver:

- **Outcome:** one observable result, suitable as a registry title.
- **Intent:** business meaning, risk, and consequence of failure.
- **Evidence sought:** command, artifact, measurement, or review that would increase confidence.
- **Technical orientation:** likely area, known locators, established conventions, and environment traps.
- **Boundaries:** likely focus, compatibilities, security or operational constraints, and open decisions.
- **Existing evidence:** commands already executed and artifact pointers.
- **Ownership:** the receiving capability and any follow-up owner.

Make every acceptance criterion observable. Name input, action, and expected result rather than vague quality labels. Scope is orientation, not a filesystem permission.

Before sending, check that sections agree, claims have evidence, the requested outcome is bounded, and the receiving role can act without reconstructing your exploration.
